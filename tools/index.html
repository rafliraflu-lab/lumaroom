Free lighting tools

Make a plan, not a guess.

These lightweight tools help you organize design choices and compare lamp ratings. They do not calculate regulated illuminance, electrical loading or code compliance.

Room lighting planner

Choose a room, its main activity, and the visual atmosphere. Get a three-layer starting plan you can test before purchasing anything.

LED label comparison calculator

Enter a lamp’s rated lumens and watts, plus daily operating hours. Compare nominal efficacy and approximate annual electricity use.

Safety note: Do not treat these outputs as electrical or building-code advice. Bulb, fixture, dimmer, voltage, wiring, and wet-location compatibility require product-specific and local requirements.
