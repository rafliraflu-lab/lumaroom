Electricity Cost Calculator

Estimate energy and operating cost for a group of fixtures.

Method and limitations
Annual kWh = count × watts × hours × 365 ÷ 1000. Excludes fixed charges, tariff tiers and standby loads.
Before you decide
Record your assumptions and test the result in the actual room. Do not use this tool for wiring, emergency lighting, wet locations or regulatory compliance.
