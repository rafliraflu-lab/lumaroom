Pendant Height Planner

Compare pendant clearance with a user-selected hanging height.

Method and limitations
Clearance above table = ceiling height − drop − table height. No universal safe height is implied; check traffic, heat and manufacturer clearances.
Before you decide
Record your assumptions and test the result in the actual room. Do not use this tool for wiring, emergency lighting, wet locations or regulatory compliance.
