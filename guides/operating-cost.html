Compare lighting energy costs over a year

Use actual rated power and operating hours rather than watt-equivalent labels.

By Saiful Bahri · Worked illustrative example

Set the comparison

Compare lamps that give similar useful light and work with the same fixture. Use electrical input watts, not the marketing replacement equivalent. Estimate separate schedules for lights used differently.

Worked example

Four 60 W lamps used four hours a day consume 350.4 kWh per year. Four 9 W lamps on the same schedule use 52.56 kWh. At 0.20 currency units per kWh, the annual difference is 59.568 currency units.

Simple payback

If the total upgrade costs 24 currency units, simple payback is about 0.40 years under these assumptions. If the lamps are used only one hour each day instead, savings fall to about 14.89 per year and payback increases to 1.61 years.

Limits

Tariff tiers, fixed charges, standby consumption, dimming and replacement life may affect total cost. Do not count a saving until useful output and compatibility are compared.
