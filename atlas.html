Lighting techniques atlas

Six original schematics showing the direction and role of light in a room.

Ambient: Broad general illumination

A ceiling source distributes light across circulation and seating. Check dark corners and direct view of the source.

Task: Localized useful light

A desk lamp directs light onto a work surface rather than the entire room. Position to limit hand shadows and screen reflections.

Accent: Focused emphasis

A spot highlights a selected surface or artwork. Check viewing angle, reflected glare and heat limits.

Cove: Indirect ceiling illumination

A concealed source aims toward the ceiling, which reflects the light. Check access for maintenance and uneven visible bands.

Under-cabinet: Counter illumination

A line source beneath a cabinet illuminates a counter without the user blocking it. Check glare from glossy countertops and product location ratings.

Wall washing: Vertical surface illumination

A source near the ceiling spreads light along a wall. Observe scalloping and texture before choosing spacing.
