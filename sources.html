Sources and further reading

These organizations explain the terminology and broad principles used throughout LumaRoom. The citations do not mean those organizations have reviewed or endorsed this site.

Brightness, efficiency and bulb selection

ENERGY STAR — Learn About Brightness. Explains lumens and familiar watt-equivalent brightness examples.

Color temperature and color rendering

U.S. Department of Energy — Purchasing Energy-Efficient Light Bulbs. Describes correlated color temperature and color rendering considerations.

ENERGY STAR — Light Fixtures. Discusses fixture types, light appearance and product selection.

Layering light

Illuminating Engineering Society — Task-Ambient Lighting. Defines complementary general and task illumination.

Illuminating Engineering Society — Momentous and Momentary. An example discussion of ambient, task and accent layers in design.

Interpretation

We draw general consumer education from these resources. Room scenarios and checklists are editorial illustrations, not standards issued by these organizations.
