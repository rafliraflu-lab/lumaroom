Lighting Layout Planner

Preview an evenly spaced ceiling grid and download the coordinates.

Method and limitations
Positions are centered in equal cells. This is a geometric draft, not a wiring plan or photometric design. Check obstructions and fixture data.
Before you decide
Record your assumptions and test the result in the actual room. Do not use this tool for wiring, emergency lighting, wet locations or regulatory compliance.
