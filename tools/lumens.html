Room Lumens Calculator

Estimate installed lumen output from target average illuminance.

Method and limitations
Required rated lumens = area × target lux ÷ (utilization × maintenance). Factors are assumptions, not measured fixture data.
Before you decide
Record your assumptions and test the result in the actual room. Do not use this tool for wiring, emergency lighting, wet locations or regulatory compliance.
