LED Savings Calculator

Compare operating costs and simple payback for a replacement.

Method and limitations
Savings = difference in annual energy × tariff; simple payback = upgrade cost ÷ positive annual savings. Compare equivalent useful light output separately.
Before you decide
Record your assumptions and test the result in the actual room. Do not use this tool for wiring, emergency lighting, wet locations or regulatory compliance.
