Draft a ceiling lighting grid

Translate a room measurement into a repeatable starting geometry.

By Saiful Bahri · Worked illustrative example

Draw first

Use inside room dimensions and mark furniture, doors, beams and areas that cannot accept a fixture. Separate task requirements from general illumination.

Worked example

A 5 × 4 m room with three columns and two rows has cells of approximately 1.67 × 2 m. Place draft centers at x = 0.83, 2.50 and 4.17 m and y = 1 and 3 m from the same corner.

Revise for activity

A grid can place one light directly behind a person standing at a counter or above a reflective screen. Mark these conflicts, then reconsider task lighting rather than adding more identical ceiling fixtures.

What the draft cannot do

A regular grid is not a wiring diagram. It does not predict glare, uniformity, ceiling structure, insulation clearance, or code compliance. Consult fixture documentation and qualified local professionals before installation.
