Questions about a guide, broken links, outdated terminology, or an accessibility issue? Send an email to rafliraflu@gmail.com.

Help us investigate a correction

Please include the page address, the statement or issue you noticed, and a source or example if you have one. We review reports and update pages when corrections are warranted; we do not promise individual technical design consultations.

Scope of replies

We cannot evaluate wiring photos, determine whether an installation meets local codes, or provide emergency electrical advice. For sparking, burning smells, damaged fittings, or circuit problems, stop using the equipment and contact a qualified professional.

Privacy

Emails you send are used to respond to and follow up on your inquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. Read the privacy notice for details.
