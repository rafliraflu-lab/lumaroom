Build a room lumen budget

Use a transparent average illuminance estimate without mistaking it for a measured design.

By Saiful Bahri · Worked illustrative example

Define the plane

A room area is not always the working plane: a desk and a kitchen counter may need separate treatment. Choose an assumed target for the plane and document why; this article does not prescribe a regulatory target.

Worked example

For a 4 × 3 m space and an illustrative target of 150 lx, 1,800 lm would need to reach the plane. With assumed utilization 0.60 and maintenance 0.80, rated fixture output is 3,750 lm. At 800 lm per fixture, round up to five.

Sensitivity check

Changing utilization from 0.60 to 0.40 raises the estimated rated output to 5,625 lm with the same target and maintenance factor. This is why choosing a factor without fixture and room information can materially change the answer.

What to verify

Check luminaire photometric data, mounting, shadows, glare and minimum illumination separately. Averages cannot guarantee acceptable light everywhere. Use a suitable meter or professional assessment when accuracy matters.
